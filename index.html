What it is

On the label. Left on the bench.

Most hydration powders are built like sports drinks. Elolyte is magnesium-first and kept simple — mixed into water, for days at a desk, not a race.

In

  • Magnesium-first electrolytes Magnesium is the point of the mix, with supporting salts as the formula settles. We will not list a fake “12 electrolytes” count until the supplier formula is locked.

Out

  • Sugar-drink theatre No neon dyes. No pile of sweeteners chasing a candy aftertaste.
